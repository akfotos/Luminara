Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

Click "Join Now" in the top navigation. Fill in your name, email, and creative discipline. You'll receive a confirmation email within a few minutes.

Yes — a basic Luminara membership is completely free. We also offer a Pro tier with advanced portfolio tools, analytics, and priority placement for featured works.

Luminara supports Photography, Design, Writing, Music & Worship, Film & Video, Fine Art, Podcasting, and Crafts & Handmade — with more categories coming.

Your Profile & Work

Once logged in, navigate to your profile dashboard and click "Add Work." You can upload images, videos, audio files, and written pieces. All submissions go through a brief review before being published.

Our editorial team curates Featured Works monthly based on quality, originality, and alignment with our community values. Being active and engaged in the community increases your visibility.

Community & Safety

All content is reviewed before publication. We use a combination of automated screening and human review to ensure everything aligns with our community guidelines rooted in respect, grace, and encouragement.

Each piece of work and each profile has a "Report" button. Alternatively, email us directly at safety@luminara.com and our team will respond within 24 hours.
