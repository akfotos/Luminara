How Luminara Works

A Faith-Aligned Creative Ecosystem

Luminara is a highly curated, faith-aligned platform designed to solve a major issue with mainstream social media: the exposure of users to explicit or harmful material while trying to find or share uplifting content.

The Blueprint

Core Architecture & Phased Rollout

Building a multi-vertical platform supporting video, audio, text, and imagery is complex. Luminara approaches this using a phased roadmap to avoid over-engineering at launch.

Phase 1

The Foundation

Focuses solely on authentication, basic profiles, video (Film), and audio (Music) pipelines. This allows the development team to iron out server loads and the upload/transcoding pipeline early.

Phase 2

Content Expansion

Introduces lighter data types like text (Poetry) and images (Visual Art/Photography), building on the stable infrastructure established in Phase 1.

Phase 3

Monetization & Engagement

Introduces monetization features (the Hire system) and social engagement loops (custom reactions), completing the full creator economy ecosystem.

Safety by Design

The Two-Layer Moderation Engine

Because Luminara promises a completely safe space for all ages, moderation isn't an afterthought — it's the core product feature. It utilizes a hybrid approach to balance speed and human oversight.

1

Automated Screening

AWS Rekognition / Google AI

Computer vision APIs scan pixels instantly for explicit content or violence, acting as an immediate digital firewall. Content that passes moves directly to publication preview; flagged content enters a temporary unlisted queue for human review.

2

Human Moderation Review

Contextual, Values-Based Compliance

Algorithms lack nuance. They cannot easily distinguish between a gratuitous depiction of violence and a sensitive artistic depiction of a biblical event (like the crucifixion). Human reviewers ensure contextual, values-based compliance before or shortly after content goes live.

Approved — Live Publication
Rejected — Triggers Strike
Under the Hood

Recommended Tech Stack

The technical architecture is optimized for a mobile-first, global audience with specific attention paid to users in regions with varying internet stability, such as Sub-Saharan Africa.

Next.js (React)

Uses Server-Side Rendering (SSR). This is crucial for creative portfolios so that search engines can easily index and rank individual artists' names and works (SEO).

PostgreSQL & Redis

PostgreSQL handles complex relational data (linking users to portfolios, collaboration posts, and metrics). Redis acts as a fast-access memory layer to cache trending feeds and handle rapid session data.

Cloudflare Stream / Mux

Rather than forcing a mobile user to download a raw 2GB video file, these services use Adaptive Bitrate Streaming. They dynamically slice video files into tiny segments, adjusting resolution on the fly based on the user's connection strength.

SendGrid / Resend Relay

To protect artist privacy, the platform uses an email relay system. When a church wants to hire a photographer, the message goes through the platform's system, hiding the artist's personal email until they choose to reply.

Design Philosophy

Culture-First Design Strategy

Unlike mainstream tech platforms that maximize user retention via conflict or standard metrics, Luminara's design direction focuses on creating a "sanctuary" feel.

Faith-Aligned Reactions

By replacing the generic "Like" button with specific buttons like Inspired, Blessed, Amen, and Beautiful, the platform shapes user behavior toward encouragement rather than superficial dopamine loops.

Inspired Blessed Amen Beautiful

Editorial Aesthetics

Using warm cream backgrounds and classic serif fonts (Playfair Display) mimics physical high-end art and literary magazines, positioning the platform as prestigious and dignified rather than corporate.
