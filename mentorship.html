What We Focus On

Four Pillars of Mentorship

Every mentorship relationship at Luminara is rooted in these four areas of growth.

Spiritual Growth

Grounded in scripture and prayer, mentors walk alongside mentees in developing a deeper, more resilient faith that fuels their creative calling.

Professional Integrity

Learn to navigate the creative industry with excellence, ethical practice, and a purpose-driven approach that honours God in every project.

Creative Development

Sharpen your craft through regular creative feedback, portfolio reviews, and skill-building sessions guided by seasoned artists in your discipline.

Community & Discipleship

Beyond the 1-on-1 relationship, mentees are invited into a broader network of peer support, group sessions, and community accountability.

The Process

How It Works

A clear, structured journey from application to transformation — in five steps.

01

Apply & Share Your Heart

Complete our intake form. Tell us about your faith walk, your creative discipline, your goals, and your availability. This helps us find the right match.

02

Get Matched

Our team prayerfully reviews every application and pairs mentees with a mentor who shares their discipline, life stage, and growth focus.

03

Introduction Call

Before the formal relationship begins, you'll have a 30-minute introductory call to ensure alignment, discuss expectations, and begin in prayer.

04

Regular Sessions

Meet bi-weekly via Zoom or in person (where available). Sessions cover creative feedback, scripture study, prayer, and accountability goals.

05

Grow & Give Back

At the end of a 6-month cycle, mentees are invited to consider becoming mentors themselves — passing the gift of sharpening forward.

What We Ask of Mentees

  • Show up consistently — attend bi-weekly sessions and come prepared
  • Be honest and open about your struggles, creative blocks, and spiritual dry seasons
  • Honour confidentiality — what is shared in the relationship stays there
  • Complete the full 6-month commitment before requesting a rematch
  • Engage in prayer and scripture between sessions

What We Ask of Mentors

  • Dedicate at least 1 hour bi-weekly to your mentee
  • Lead with humility — share your failures as freely as your successes
  • Pray for your mentee regularly, not just during sessions
  • Maintain strict confidentiality about all personal disclosures
  • Attend quarterly mentor check-ins with the Luminara team
